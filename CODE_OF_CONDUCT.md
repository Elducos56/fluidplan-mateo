# Code of conduct

fluidplan follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior bothers you, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/morganhub/fluidplan/security/advisories/new) (choose it even though the form talks about vulnerabilities) or through the contact options on the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/morganhub). Reports are read in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues, or block contributors, when they do not respect this code of conduct.
